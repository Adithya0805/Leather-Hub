## Verified Fact Registry
1. **Town of Export Excellence**: Recognized as a Town of Export Excellence (TEE) for leather by the Government of India. *(Source: DGFT Foreign Trade Policy, 2009)*
2. **Tamil Nadu Export Share**: Tamil Nadu accounts for 40%–45% of India's leather exports, with the Ambur/Palar cluster as a primary producer. *(Source: Council for Leather Exports / CLE Annual Report, 2023)*
3. **National Sector Export**: Indian leather & footwear sector achieved ~US$ 5.7B export earnings in FY25. *(Source: Council for Leather Exports / CLE, 2025)*
4. **Employment & Demographics**: The industry employs ~4.4M workers across India, with ~30% female participation. *(Source: Ministry of Commerce & Industry / CLE, 2024)*
5. **Research Anchor**: Central Leather Research Institute (CSIR-CLRI), world's largest leather R&D institute supporting Ambur tanners, was founded April 24, 1948. *(Source: CSIR-CLRI Profile, 1948/2023)*
6. **Eco Compliance**: Ambur tanneries operate Common Effluent Treatment Plants (CETPs) with Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD). *(Source: Tamil Nadu Pollution Control Board / TNPCB, 2018)*
7. **Tanning Origins**: Commercial tanning in the Ambur/Palar basin dates back to c. 1900–1905, shifting to finished leather exports in the 1970s–1980s. *(Source: North Arcot District Gazetteer, 1981)*
